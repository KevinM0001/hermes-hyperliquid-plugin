## B1 (BLOCKING): an unknown level size does not invalidate the largest-level claim

> `ladder_rows` removes malformed levels, then computes `largest` over the surviving rows. … The chart independently repeats that selection and draws a "largest returned ask level" line. … Required correction: withhold the affected side's largest-level value with a reason when missing price/size prevents the ranking from being established, and omit its largest-level chart line. Add a regression for malformed non-top levels.

**Agreed; fixed.**

**What changed.**
- **`hl_desk/book.py` `ladder_rows`.** When any returned level on a side has a missing or invalid px or sz, that side's `largest` is `null`, with `bid_reason` / `ask_reason`: "N returned ask level(s) have a missing or invalid px or sz, so the largest returned level can't be established". The other side is unaffected.
- **`hl_desk/state.py` `_chart`.** The chart no longer ranks rows itself. It draws the largest-level lines from `ladder.largest`, so a withheld side has no line, and the reason is listed in `price_lines_withheld`. The serving-time and on-screen aging (`serving_public.py`, `app.js` `ageShown`) now add to that list instead of replacing it.
- **Schema.** `book.largest_level` and `chart.price_line` state the rule. SNAPSHOT.md does too.

**Evidence.** `tests/test_h1_review3.py::test_b1_a_malformed_level_withholds_that_sides_largest_and_its_chart_line` is your reproduction: bids 99.9 × 1 and 99.8 × 2; asks 100.1 × 1, 100.2 with sz missing, and 100.3 × 3. The book is fed through the desk as a fresh 20-level push and built into a snapshot.
- `largest.ask` is null with the reason.
- `largest.bid` is 99.8 × 2.
- The chart has a largest-bid line and no largest-ask line, and `price_lines_withheld` gives the reason.
- The actual `hl_market` handler returns the same.
