## 2. BLOCKING: "A malformed-trade gap retains the previous segment's freshness and publishes fresh zeros."

**Changed.** Every coverage boundary inside a connection now goes through one path, `MarketState._after_boundary` (`hl_desk/state.py:171`), called at lines 147 and 168. It ends any Flow Deck regime at the gap, restarts the deck's segment, and restarts trade freshness with `FieldTracker.restart` (`hl_desk/freshness.py:49`). The restart clears the earlier progress time, source time and watermark.
- **Until the next valid trade.** The trades field is `warming_up`, and its reason names the gap: "no verified progress since the coverage gap at … (malformed trade (missing or invalid side))" (`hl_desk/freshness.py:98`). So flow values, CVD, large prints, wallet panels and the Flow Deck card are null with that reason, never fresh zeros.
- **After it.** The first valid trade that advances the new segment clears the restart (line 58) and makes progress again.

**Test.** `test_malformed_gap_restarts_freshness_before_any_new_trade` is Codex's case: BTC opened at T0, a valid $100 buy at T0+1 s, a trade missing `side` at T0+2 s, and a build at T0+2.2 s. The build has trades `warming_up`, flow values and wallet sides null with the reason, and no regime. After a valid trade at T0+3 s, trades are fresh and volume counts only that trade.

## 3. BLOCKING: "The real dispatcher silently drops malformed trades before the coverage validator can see them."

**Changed.** `feed.dispatch` now hands every trades message to `dispatch_trades` (`hl_desk/feed.py:44`), which routes by each record's coin, never by the first record's.
- **One subscribed coin.** A message naming one subscribed coin goes to that market whole. Its records that name no coin or another coin reach the market's validator as invalid. `trade_problems` (`hl_desk/trades.py:71`) now also requires the coin to equal the subscription's coin, which `TradeStore.on_message` checks (line 148). They end the coverage segment as a gap, as in finding 3 of review 2.
- **More than one.** A message naming more than one subscribed coin, which shouldn't happen since trades are subscribed per coin, is split by coin. Each market also receives the records it can't place.
- **No subscribed coin.** A message that names no subscribed coin, but has a record without one, can't be attributed. Every market's `on_unattributed_trades` (`hl_desk/state.py:162`) then ends its open segment with `TradeStore.force_boundary` (`hl_desk/trades.py:182`). The reason is "a trades message with no attributable coin", the `unattributed_messages` counter goes up, and freshness restarts as in finding 2. A market still waiting for its subscription snapshot isn't counting yet, so it is left alone.

**Tests.**
- `test_dispatcher_routes_a_message_whose_first_record_has_no_coin` is Codex's case, run through `feed.dispatch`. After a counted $100 trade, a message arrives whose first record lacks `coin`, with a valid $200 trade second, then a valid $100 trade at T0+3 s. The malformed counter is 1, the gap's reason names `coin`, the window starts after the bad message, and volume is $100.
- `test_unattributable_trades_message_ends_every_markets_coverage`: BTC and HYPE.
- `test_record_naming_another_coin_is_invalid_for_the_subscription`.

## 4. BLOCKING: "Wallet freshness excludes time spent fetching the account and orders."

**Changed.**
- **Arrival times.** `fetch_wallet_current` (`hl_desk/info_client.py:35`) records four times: `requested_ms` before the first request, `state_received_ms` and `orders_received_ms` as each answer arrives, and `returned_ms` when both are in hand.
- **Age at serving.** `current_account` (`hl_desk/tools.py:299`) judges the account's age at `returned_ms` (line 302), so network delay and the wait for the open-orders answer count as age. `account_freshness` (line 273) applies the 10 s limit under the clock bound, and an answer without a recorded returned time is unverified.
- **Output.** The result carries all four times. The open-orders reason gives that answer's own arrival time.
- **Tab.** It shows "both answers in hand at …" and "… s old when served, request time included" (`dashboard/src/app.js:1395`, `1410`).
- **Schema.** `freshness.fields.clearinghouseState` states the rule.

**Tests.**
- `test_slow_account_requests_count_as_age` is Codex's case. It patches the clock and makes each request take 9 s, against a source time 5 s before the first request. The account is 23.0 s old, `delayed`, and its values are null.
- `test_account_without_a_serving_time_is_unverified`.

**Live, in Hermes v0.23.0 at `2a0ed13`.** A wallet opened from the BTC panel showed "account state fresh · … · 0.575 s old when served, request time included (limit 10 s)". Its open orders showed as unavailable, with the time that answer arrived (`evidence/screenshots/review3-wallet-drilldown-live.png`).

## DEFERRED 1: "Numeric book validation accepts impossible levels as covered depth."

**Changed.**
- **Rejected levels.** `parse_levels` (`hl_desk/book.py:30`) rejects a level whose price or size is missing, not a number, or not positive (line 40). It is never summed, so that side's totals are unavailable with a reason, and a bad best level makes the mid unknown.
- **API count.** `_api_count` (line 18) accepts only a whole number ≥ 0. A fractional or negative count is null, not truncated.
