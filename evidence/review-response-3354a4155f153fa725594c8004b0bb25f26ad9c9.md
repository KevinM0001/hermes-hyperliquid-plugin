## B2 (BLOCKING): an incomplete node position effect becomes a verified zero bucket

> An effect dictionary is accepted if its supplied values parse. The parser does not require the side's complete pair of components or verify that they account for the fill. … a complete aggressive buy at price 100, size 4, with taker effect `{"cover":"3"}` … Actual: cover $300 / 75%, new long $0 / 0%, unclassified $0, `available: true`.

**Agreed; fixed.**

**What changed.** `hl_desk/node/flow.py` `parse_effect`, used by `parse_exec` for every participant, accepts an effect only when it is complete for the participant's side:
- a buy has both `cover` and `new_long`, a sell both `exit` and `new_short` (PROTOCOL.md 4.6), and no other component;
- none is negative;
- the two add up exactly to the fill size.

Otherwise the effect is unavailable, with the reason. That makes the Flow Deck and burst buckets, and the wallet splits, treat its notional as unclassified, with the side unavailable and the reason, never a zero bucket. Schema `node.activity_buckets` and `deck.activity_buckets` say so.

**Evidence.** `tests/test_h1_review4.py`:
- `test_b2_only_complete_effects_count`: a missing component, the other side's key, a negative component and a non-conserving pair are each rejected with their reason.
- `test_b2_an_incomplete_effect_is_unclassified_never_a_zero_bucket` is your case: buy 4 at 100 with taker effect `{"cover":"3"}`. The buying side is unavailable with "new_long missing", with $400 unclassified and no buckets. The valid flip control (cover 3, new long 1) gives $300 / $100 and 75% / 25%.
- Every recorded fixture still passes the stricter check: the full suite at `0d7829403d1c72faad6ffd323b59fdfe566ef5a6` passes.
